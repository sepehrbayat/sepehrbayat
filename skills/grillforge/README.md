# GrillForge

**Put the idea on the grill before reality does.**

Created by [Sepehr Bayat](https://github.com/sepehrbayat).

GrillForge is an original adversarial decision-making skill. It turns a vague idea into explicit claims, directs the most heat toward the assumptions with the largest blast radius, and forces a practical verdict:

- **Advance**
- **Probe**
- **Pivot**
- **Kill**

## The method

GrillForge runs through six original stations:

1. **Claim Stack** — identifies everything that must be true.
2. **Heat Map** — ranks claims by blast radius, cost of error, irreversibility, and evidence gap.
3. **Five Flames** — attacks substitution, incentives, friction, scale, and unrealistic behavior.
4. **Flip Tests** — exposes bias with counterfactual reversals.
5. **Proof Skewers** — converts decisive unknowns into cheap falsifiable tests.
6. **Final Plate** — delivers a hard verdict, next move, and stop condition.

## Installation

Copy `SKILL.md` into your agent's skills directory:

```text
skills/
└── grillforge/
    └── SKILL.md
```

Invoke it with prompts such as:

```text
Use GrillForge to pressure-test my SaaS idea.
```

```text
Put this product strategy on the grill.
```

```text
Run the full GrillForge process and tell me what survives.
```

## Best use cases

- product and startup ideas;
- feature prioritization;
- business models;
- go-to-market strategies;
- architecture decisions;
- expensive or difficult-to-reverse choices;
- projects with high confidence but weak evidence.

## Philosophy

Most weak ideas are not destroyed by competition. They survive internally because nobody applies enough pressure to their foundational claims.

GrillForge applies the heat before the market, users, cost, or complexity do.
